# 0029 — Bitwise Operations

Compute bitwise AND, OR, and XOR on `6` and `3`, plus a left shift of `6` by one bit, printing `and: 2`, `or: 7`, `xor: 5`, and `shift: 12`. Go uses `&`, `|`, `^`, and `<<` for bitwise work (with `^` doubling as unary NOT and `&^` for AND-NOT). They require integer operands of matching type; `6 << 1` doubles to 12.
